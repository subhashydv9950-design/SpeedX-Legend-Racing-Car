About SPEEDX LEGEND

Our Vision

SPEEDX LEGEND is an independent racing game project focused on original fictional cars, connected open-world environments, driving challenges and player progression.

FOUNDER & DEVELOPER

Subhash Yadav

Creator and developer of the SPEEDX LEGEND project.

This project is being developed step by step for the web, with mobile devices as an important target.

Planned Features

• Connected open-world driving

• Sequential career levels across different worlds

• Original fictional cars and upgrades

• Multiple interface languages

• Mobile-friendly driving controls
